Our Story

Proudly Nigerian. Proudly African. AmulRee Fashion was built to bring vibrant Ankara-inspired style to every wardrobe.

Rooted in Lagos, Made for the World

AmulRee Fashion started with a simple idea: Nigerian style deserves a global stage. Founded in Lagos, we set out to create a wardrobe brand that celebrates the boldness of Ankara prints while staying true to modern, everyday wearability.

Every piece we release is chosen with care — from the fabric to the fit — because we believe fashion should make you feel confident, comfortable, and connected to where you're from.

Today, AmulRee Fashion serves customers across Nigeria, with a growing collection that blends traditional African textiles with contemporary silhouettes for work, weekends, and everything in between.
